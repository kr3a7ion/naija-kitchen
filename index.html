Party Jollof Rice

Serves 6 · 45 mnutes · Medium

Spicy One pot

The smoky, tomato-rich rice that starts arguments at every family gathering. This is the party version, cooked long enough to catch at the bottom.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long grain parboiled rice
  • 6 large tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, diced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • Half cup vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 stock cubes
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and half the onion until smooth
  2. Heat the oil and fry the remaining onion until golden
  3. Add the tomato paste and fry for 2 minutes
  4. Add the blended tomato mixture and fry for 10 minutes
  5. Add the stock cubes, curry powder, thyme and salt. Stir and cook for 5 minutes
  6. Add the rice and stir to coat with the sauce
  7. Add 4 cups of water and bring to a boil
  8. Reduce the heat to low, cover and cook for 20 minutes
  9. Remove from heat and let it sit for 10 minutes before serving

Notes

The party smoke: the slight catch at the bottom is the whole point. Resist the urge to stir while it cooks. That bottom layer is what makes party jollof taste like a party.
